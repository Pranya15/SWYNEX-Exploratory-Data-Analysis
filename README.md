# SWYNEX – Exploratory Data Analysis

## Project Overview
This project focuses on Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) using the Customer Personality Analysis dataset.

The objective is to understand customer demographics, spending behaviour, purchasing patterns and marketing campaign performance.

## Tools and Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Analysis Performed
1. Dataset exploration and descriptive statistics.
2. Customer income and age analysis.
3. Customer spending analysis.
4. Shopping channel analysis.
5. Marketing campaign performance analysis.
6. Education and marital status analysis.
7. Correlation analysis.
8. Identification of unusual values and potential anomalies.

## Key Business Insights

1. **Product Spending:** Wine recorded the highest total spending, followed by meat products.
2. **Shopping Channels:** Physical stores recorded the highest number of purchases.
3. **Income and Spending:** Income showed a positive correlation of approximately 0.665 with total customer spending.
4. **Marketing Campaigns:** The latest campaign response rate was 14.91%, higher than the five previous campaign acceptance rates.
5. **Customer Demographics:** The largest age group was 46–55 years, while Graduation was the most common education category.

## Anomalies Identified
- Three unusual birth years were flagged for review.
- The maximum recorded income of 666,666 was identified for further investigation.

## Project Files
- `SWYNEX-Exploratory-Data-Analysis.ipynb` – Python analysis and visualisations.
- `cleaned_customer_data.csv` – Cleaned dataset.
- `charts/` – Visualisations generated during analysis.

## Conclusion
This project helped identify customer spending patterns, preferred shopping channels, demographic trends and marketing campaign performance. The findings can help businesses understand their customers and make more informed marketing decisions.
